Vaultlings

Privacy Policy

Last updated 12 August 2026

Vaultlings does not collect any personal information from anyone, child or adult. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising and no analytics. The app does not send information anywhere, because it has no way to: it makes no network connections at all.

What the app stores

Vaultlings saves your progress — the name you gave your Vaultling, which creature you chose, its happiness and hunger, your streak, the amounts in your three jars, and what you have bought — on the device itself. This information never leaves the device. It is not backed up to us, because there is no us to back it up to: we operate no servers and receive nothing.

Deleting the app deletes all of it.

The dollar amounts in the app

The money shown in the jars is a record a child keeps of pocket money they have been given in real life. It is not connected to any bank, card or payment account. Vaultlings has no access to any real financial account and cannot move real money.

Motion

The app reads the phone's motion sensor for one purpose: when you tilt the device, the picture of your Vaultling's den shifts slightly, so the room feels like a place. Those readings are used in the moment to draw the screen and are never recorded, stored or transmitted. If you have Reduce Motion switched on in iOS accessibility settings, the app does not read the sensor at all.

Children

Vaultlings is made for children. We have designed it so that the question of children's data does not arise: the app collects no personal information, asks for no email address, has no chat, no social features, no user-generated content, no links out to the web, and no advertising of any kind. Nothing a child types — including the name they give their Vaultling — is ever sent anywhere.

Actions involving real money or account settings are placed behind a grown-up check that a young child is not expected to pass.

Purchases

If Vaultlings offers optional paid items in the future, those purchases will be handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. Vaultlings never sees or stores card numbers, billing addresses or any other payment details. Gems, which are earned by playing, can never be bought with money, and money can never be bought with gems.

Third parties

Vaultlings includes no advertising networks, no analytics services, no crash reporting services and no social media kits. No third party receives any information about you from this app.

Beta testing

While Vaultlings is distributed for testing through Apple's TestFlight, Apple collects information about testers and their devices under its own policies, including crash logs and, if a tester chooses to send one, feedback. That collection is Apple's, governed by Apple's Privacy Policy, and is not something Vaultlings controls or adds to.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date at the top. If a future version of the app ever collected personal information — which is not the plan — that change would be described here before it took effect.
